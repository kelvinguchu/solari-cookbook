---
title: replay
description: Re-run a saved reproducer and check that the recorded signature still appears.
---

```bash
flakelab replay <reproducer> [options]
```

Validates a reproducer, re-executes its exact fault set, and checks that the recorded
normalized failure signature still appears.

| Option | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--concurrency <n>` | `1` | Playwright workers used for replay trials. |

## Why replay matters

The reproducer is the artifact that makes a flake reviewable. Replay is how a colleague, a
CI job, or a future you confirms that the recipe still reproduces the same failure - on a
different machine, at a different time, after unrelated changes.

Exit code `1` means the reproducer did not reproduce, either because no failure occurred or
because a *different* failure signature appeared.

```bash
flakelab replay flakelab.repro.yaml --concurrency 4
```
